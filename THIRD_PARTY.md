## Provenance and rejected references
`data/illustrations.json` and `assets/illustrations/manifest.json` record the provider, creator, asset ID, original source URL, pinned version, download URL, local path, SHA-256, license, and review status. Approving a new candidate is scoped to its word ID and hash. Private device decisions are not included in the public catalog.

Former ARASAAC toothpaste4197 and cupboard37149 references are rejected; no ARASAAC artwork is bundled or requested. Their historical attribution was Sergio Palao, Government of Aragon, ARASAAC, CC BY-NC-SA. Nothing here changes ARASAAC's terms of use.

The R application icon comes from the original supplied app; smaller sizes are resized copies. No font files are included. These artwork licenses do not assign a blanket license to application code or establish redistribution rights for the supplied vocabulary or original PDF. Learner backups and personal review exports must not be published.
