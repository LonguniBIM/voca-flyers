Source & important distinctions

The original document includes alternatives (=), contrasts (#), singular/plural pairs (/), optional forms and words with different meanings. The library preserves the raw source references, expands explicit forms, and keeps meanings separate. A mixed session still avoids repeating a spelling.

Examples such as crocodile/alligator, city/town and forest/jungle are grouped in the source; this app does not certify them as interchangeable in every context. Source spelling and glosses are not silently corrected. Names are optional listening dictation. The Speaking appendix is a reference only, with no automatic assessment.
